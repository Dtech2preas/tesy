Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Information Collection

D-TECH uni check is designed to be privacy-first. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal academic data (such as your subjects and marks) to our servers. All academic calculations are performed locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Advertising

Because our application relies on advertising to remain free, we integrate third-party ad networks. These third parties may collect anonymous, non-personal data to serve relevant ads. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location data (city/country level)
  • App usage statistics

We do not share your entered academic data with these ad networks.

3. Local Storage

The app uses your device's local storage to save your entered subjects, marks, and preferences so you do not have to re-enter them each time you open the app. This data never leaves your device.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes.
