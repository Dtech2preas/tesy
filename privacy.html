Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Information Collection

D-TECH uni check is designed to be privacy-first. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal academic data (such as your subjects and marks) to our servers. All academic calculations are performed locally on your device.

2. No Advertising or Tracking

This app does not contain any advertisements, third-party trackers, or analytics scripts. Your usage of the app is completely private.

3. Local Storage

The app uses your device's local storage to save your entered subjects, marks, and preferences so you do not have to re-enter them each time you open the app. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your device settings.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes.
